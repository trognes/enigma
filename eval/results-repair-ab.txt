THE READING
===========

1. try_repair COSTS 3-7% OF A CLIMB ON THE CPU, NOT 16.9%.  The GPU
   probes put try_repair plus the outer loop at 16.9% of a natural climb
   at L~105 (results-gpu-ablation-m1.txt run 2); the CPU pays 3.3% at
   L=40, 4.6% at 80 and 7.2% at 100.  So a GPU lane and a CPU core price
   a convergence scan differently by about 3x, which is what the
   cross-hardware caveat in CLAUDE.md's --no-repair entry anticipated.
   It is cheap, but "~zero cost" overstates it at the top of the range.

2. THE L=60 COST ROW IS CONTAMINATED, AS PREDICTED BEFORE THE RECOVERY
   TABLE WAS READ.  Its 1.337 sits between neighbours reading 1.046 and
   1.033, and its `on` cost (0.247) exceeds both L=80's (0.223) and
   L=100's (0.237) -- backwards for a climb linear in L.  The `off`
   column is cleanly monotone (0.172, 0.185, 0.213, 0.221), so the
   well-behaved arm is the one saying the other is wrong.  Re-timing the
   IDENTICAL fixtures twice reads 1.069 and 1.099.  The true R' at L=60
   is 9, not 11, so the only apparently-significant matched-time cell
   (p = 0.053) rests on a 37% restart bonus where ~9% was earned.  It is
   an artefact and is not a finding.

3. THE RE-PAIR EARNS ITS KEEP AT L>=80, DECISIVELY, AT MATCHED RESTARTS:
   423 breaks against 381 at L=80 (62 on-only against 20, p = 0.000) and
   738 against 684 at L=100 (87 against 33, p = 0.000).  At L=40 nothing
   resolves -- 26 against 22 of 2000, p = 0.424 -- which is the documented
   floor effect, not evidence of absence: at forty letters 1.3% of trials
   break at all.

4. NO LENGTH SHOWS THE RE-PAIR LOSING.  So ENHANCEMENTS.md item 19
   closes with NO CHANGE: it stays always-on and is not length-gated.

5. THE VALUE QUESTION IS ONLY HALF-TESTED, AND THE REASON IS INTEGER
   RESTARTS.  At -R 8 a 3-7% saving cannot buy a restart: round(8 x
   1.033) and round(8 x 1.046) are both 8, so at L=40 and L=80 the `off+`
   arm ran the IDENTICAL command to `off` and its column is a copy --
   which the identical discordant counts (9/5 and 62/20) confirm, and
   which doubles as a check that the pairing and the determinism are
   sound.  Only L=100 got a real bonus (R' = 9, +12.5%, itself slightly
   over-credited), where `on` leads 738 to 719 at p = 0.135.  A proper
   matched-time test needs a budget where the ratio is expressible: at
   -R 64 the measured ratios give R' = 66, 69, 67 -- a genuine 3-7%.
   Until that is run, "the re-pair is worth more than the restarts it
   costs" is supported at L=80 (where it costs no restart at all) and
   merely not contradicted at L=100.

WHAT THE HARNESS LEARNED.  min-of-2 reps was too thin for a slope that is
a difference of two sums: one bad timing in four rows produced one false
positive at p = 0.053.  The pilot now takes min of 5, and the cost table
marks the two readings a reader would otherwise take at face value -- a
ratio below 1.0 (impossible, since the re-pair only ever adds work, so
it is the timer winning) and a ratio that rounds to no extra restart, in
which case `off+` is the same command as `off` and its column is a copy
rather than an arm.  The rows above were produced before that change.
