Refinement shape equivalence across MACHINE VARIANTS
====================================================

eval/ring_stride_refine_shape_probe.py --trials 100 --lengths 60,110 --seed 11
  --k {2,3,5}, once without --m4 and once with.  Default --wheels 8, so the
  three-wheel draw comes from I-VIII.

WHY THIS RUN EXISTS.  The equivalence measurement behind the derived refinement
(refinement.md 11) was wheels I-V on the standard machine.  The two machine
features the derivation has to survive were argued from the design, not
measured: two-notch right wheels (VI-VIII), which step the middle wheel twice
per revolution and so change how often a shifted schedule straddles the middle
wheel's own notch; and M4, whose static Greek wheel changes the substitution but
not the stepping.  This closes that gap.

It also measures the shape that actually SHIPPED.  Until now the probe scored
only the shapes that led to the derivation, and the equivalence claim rested on
the derived set being a subset of the measured-clean "lock-start2".  That bounds
it the wrong way -- a subset can lose where its superset does not -- so
"derived" is now scored directly: ring1 computed from the step-count drift
between the coarse winner's schedule and the candidate's, exactly as enigma.cc
does it.

REALIZED TWO-NOTCH COVERAGE (replayed from the seeded stream, not an
expectation).  Per arm, over the 200 trials each K value shares:

  standard   any two-notch wheel 162/200   two-notch RIGHT wheel 73/200
  M4         any two-notch wheel 165/200   two-notch RIGHT wheel 87/200

Across the three K values that is 1200 trials in all, 480 of them with a
two-notch wheel in the position that drives the middle wheel.

COLUMNS.  "coarse ok" = the coarse pass alone already decoded the truth.  The
shape columns = that shape's best-scoring candidate decodes the truth (or the
coarse winner already did).  "lost:X" = trials the enumeration ("shipped")
recovers and shape X does not -- the equivalence metric.

RESULT.  lost:derived = 0 in all twelve cells, and "derived" matches "shipped"
recovery percentage for percentage everywhere -- on the standard machine and on
M4, at K=2/3/5, at both lengths.  1200 paired trials.

The run has the resolution to have detected a loss: the cheaper shapes below
"derived" lose 1-9 trials per cell (49 in total across lock-off1, shift2,
lock-both and lock-all), so a zero here is a measurement, not an absence of
signal.  "lock-start2" also loses nothing, as it did on wheels I-V -- expected,
since it is a superset of "derived"; the point of scoring "derived" separately
is that this fact was never evidence for it.

The two arms differ in difficulty as their construction predicts and not
otherwise: M4's coarse pass is a little better at L=110 (68/51/36% vs
62/35/30%) and its refined recovery a little higher (91% vs 85%), which is the
substitution being different, not the stepping.  The stepping-sensitive
quantity -- whether the derivation finds the right ring1 -- is identical.
