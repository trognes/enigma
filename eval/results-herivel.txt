The Herivel tip is alive in the 1941 corpus
==========================================

Question.  A survey of every readable open Enigma-breaking implementation
(enigma-suite / Enigma@Home, enigma-cuda, Lasry's CrypTool 2 analyzer, bomm,
and four smaller GitHub projects) found nothing ciphertext-only that this
tool lacks: all are IC -> bigram -> trigram swap climbs with swap-order or
kick diversification, partial (E-)stecker exhaustion, and ring collapses the
repo has in exact form.  The one lever none of them uses is not a ciphertext
statistic at all.  It is an OPERATOR HABIT, Herivel's 1940 tip: an operator
sets the day's rings, closes the lid, and the Grundstellung of his first
message sits at or near the ring, because the rings are what he last saw in
the windows.  Bletchley read the day's ring off the cluster of early
Grundstellungen.  Is it alive in this corpus?

Method.  eval/herivel_probe.py.  The corpus carries both halves -- the
indicator (Grundstellung, sent in clear) for 54 messages and the published
day-key ring for their dates -- so the tip is testable directly.  Cyclic
distance |Grundstellung - ring| per wheel; uniform gives mean 6.5,
P(d <= 2) = 19.2%, all three wheels within +-1 = (3/26)^3 = 0.15%.  Ring 0 is
a class representative on three recovered days, so wheel 0 is scored on
published rings only.  Send times come from the message-header page.

    python3 eval/herivel_probe.py

Findings
--------

1. IT IS ALIVE.  Four messages sit within +-1 of the day's ring on ALL
   THREE wheels against 0.11 expected -- p ~ 1e-5 -- and every one is on a
   PUBLISHED ring (PKF, LSB, YHO, JQH), so this is not an artefact of a
   class-representative ring.  On the two wheels a sweep would pin, 6 of 54
   messages (11%) are within +-1 against 1.3% chance, p ~ 1e-4.

2. THE RIGHT WHEEL CARRIES MOST OF IT.  Wheels 0 and 1 are near uniform in
   the aggregate (23%, 22% within 2) while wheel 2 reads 31.5%, mean 5.2
   against 6.5.  The rightmost ring is the last one set and the nearest to
   the operator; where the tip holds partially it is the right ring that
   stays.

3. BY SEND TIME IT LOOKS LIKE HERIVEL DESCRIBED IT.  Of the 8 days with a
   timed first message, 3 are hits; of the 5 later messages on those days,
   none.  Small numbers -- this is suggestive, not established -- but the
   pattern is the historical one, and it says the Grundstellung of a day's
   EARLIEST message is the one to read.  Time of day is not decisive: the
   hits were sent at 0740, 1450 and 2305.

4. A CONSTRAINED SWEEP FOLLOWS, and it is cheap.  For an unbroken message
   whose Grundstellung is G, pin ring1 and ring2 to G +-1 (9 combinations;
   ring0 is unidentifiable and stays the offset axis) and sweep everything
   else exactly.  For RXPSB (Grundstellung ZDN, reflector B, wheels I-V):

     9 x 60 wheel orders x 17 576 starts = 9 491 040 keys, exact rings

   against the 57.7M keys of a stride-3 full sweep.  Measured on this box:
   one ring-pinned wheel order (17 576 keys) climbs in 2.1 s on 4 threads at
   -R 0, i.e. ~8 250 key-climbs/s.  So -R 16 is ~5 h here; -R 500 is ~6.6
   DAYS here -- and, relative to the 57.7M x -R 500 run already made on the
   message, the same compute buys -R 3000 on the Herivel rings, six times
   the restarts where the L=107 climb curve is still rising steeply.

5. A SECOND, SEPARATE BENEFIT: THE BAR DROPS.  The best-of-K bar is
   sqrt(2 ln K): 6.21 at 230M keys, 6.00 at 57.7M, 5.67 at 9.49M.  RXPSB's
   true-key z sits at the bar (real traffic reads ~6 at 107 letters), so a
   third of a sigma is worth having on discrimination, independent of the
   compute.

6. THE PRIOR IS THE WHOLE COST.  Around 11% of messages satisfy the +-1
   condition, more among first-of-day messages and less for one sent at
   1020 like RXPSB.  Call it 10-15%.  That is a lottery ticket -- but a
   better one than a reflector-C sweep (a few-percent prior at ~30% power),
   and the ticket costs a sixth of a full sweep.  +-2 on both rings (25
   combinations, 2.8x) is the fallback.

7. FOR BYQMZ IT BUYS NOTHING NEW.  Its Grundstellung MGS was sent at 0014,
   fourteen minutes after the key change -- the strongest Herivel position
   in the set -- but the full reflector-B sweep at -R 100 already covered
   every ring exactly.  The tip would only make the non-standard-kit
   contingencies (reflector C, wheels VI-VIII) overnight runs instead of
   multi-day ones, and their priors are too small to justify even that.

What is running
---------------

A first-pass RXPSB sweep on the nine Herivel ring combinations, centre (D,N)
first, -R 16, reflector B, wheels I-V, all starts, --confidence 256
--doubling-report 7 --seed-dedup, ~5 h on this 4-thread box.  The high-R
version belongs on a faster box; per combination, for rr in
DN CN EN DM DO CM CO EM EO:

    ./enigma -u B -w ... -r Arr -g ... -c -K --polish -S k4f10 -f \
        -l wehrmacht -R 2000 -T 7 --confidence 256 --doubling-report 7 \
        --seed-dedup < rxpsb.txt

with the ciphertext carrying its two garble dashes as placeholder letters
and the Kenngruppe stripped.  Validate any candidate with
eval/day_key_from_indicator.py --msg START:ZDN:QMF.
