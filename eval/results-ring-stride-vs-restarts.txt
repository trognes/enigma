--ring-stride vs more -R restarts, at MATCHED WALL TIME
=======================================================

Harness : eval/ring_stride_vs_restarts.py
Command : TRIALS=40 THREADS=4 \
          CELLS="100:10:8:1.40:mid:2 100:10:8:1.86:mid:3" \
          python3 eval/ring_stride_vs_restarts.py

Setup
-----
Authentic telegraphic German excerpts (eval/enigma-messages.txt,
eval/enigma-army-messages-1941.txt), L=100 letters, a random 10-pair
plugboard HIDDEN from the search, reflector and wheel order given.

  search : -c -J --polish -f -l wehrmacht -u B -w 123 -r A.. -g A.. -T 4
  base   : -R 8
  stride : -R round(8 * ratio) --ring-stride K

ring1 AND start1 wildcarded (102076 keys). This shape is REQUIRED: the
refinement costs 25 * rc[1] * gc[1] * 26 keys, times -R like everything
else, and with ring1 pinned -- the tool's own default -r "AA." -- that
fixed cost exceeds the coarse saving and --ring-stride costs MORE than it
saves. Measured at L=100, -R 20, K=2:

    -r AA. -g A..     17576 keys unstrided ->  25688 strided   loses
    -r A.. -g AK.     17576 keys unstrided ->  25688 strided   loses
    -r A.. -g A..    102076 keys unstrided ->  69913 strided   1.46x

Ratios are the measured equal-R wall-time ratios on that shape, 1.40
(K=2) and 1.86 (K=3), against key-count ratios of 1.46 and 1.88. Both
the coarse pass and the refinement scale with -R, so the ratio does not
depend on R.

Results (40 paired trials per cell, ENIGMA_SEED=0)
--------------------------------------------------
    K   R_base  R_str   base_pc  str_pc   base_ex  str_ex   b-only  s-only
    2   8       11      29.8%    25.6%    4        4        3       3
    3   8       15      29.8%    30.0%    4        5        3       4

    K=2 paired stride-base  -4.2 pp, 95% CI [-13.3, +4.9]
                            -> no detectable difference
    K=3 paired stride-base  +0.2 pp
                            -> no detectable difference

Wall seconds per arm: 3036/2867 (K=2), 3028/2994 (K=3) -- matched to
within 6% and 1%, with the stride arm if anything slightly under-served.

Conclusion
----------
NO DETECTABLE DIFFERENCE at either K. Spending the stride's saving on
more -R restarts neither beats nor loses to an exhaustive ring2 sweep.

Read the CI before drawing a stronger conclusion: +-9pp at n=40 rules out
a LARGE effect in either direction and nothing smaller. Judged on the
means alone the K=2 cell looks like a 4.2pp loss; its exact recovery is
tied 4/4 and its paired wins split 3-3, and the interval spans zero.
Resolving a ~2pp effect here would need roughly 20x the trials, which at
~75 s per arm per trial is not worth spending on a flag that is not
recommended by default.

The base arm is identical in both cells (29.8%, 4 exact) because the
trial RNG does not depend on K and ENIGMA_SEED is pinned -- the two cells
posed the same 40 problems. That is a determinism check, not a
coincidence.
