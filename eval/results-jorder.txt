ORDERING -J's MOVE SCAN BY IC INSTEAD OF BY THE TARGET MODEL
============================================================

  python3 eval/jorder_ab.py --trials 400 --length {100,167} --seed {4242,99}

  -f -l wehrmacht -c -J -S k4f10 -R 8, 10-pair board hidden, rotor key
  given, authentic HG Nord decrypts, 1600 paired trials over two seeds.
  The arms differ ONLY by $ENIGMA_JORDER=ic.

  L      mean ic-target   exact (ic/target of 800)   McNemar z   compute
  100        -0.03pp            216 / 217              +0.10      -10.9%
  167        +1.18pp            617 / 605              -1.29      -10.8%

VERDICT: recovery is INDISTINGUISHABLE, compute is ~11% lower.


WHAT THE CHANGE IS
------------------

-J visits the 325 plugboard toggles best-first, rebuilding that order once
per restart by scoring every move from the perturbed starting board.  With a
FUSED OR QUAD TARGET each of those 325 probes is a full decode -- probe_toggle
only takes the O(26) histogram path when hist_model() is true, and that is
IC, mono and monoic only.  So the recommended recipe pays the scan in full.

Measured at -f -c -S f10 -R 64 with a 10-pair board, the scan is

    20 800 of 91 451 plugboards scored at L=100   (22.7%)
    20 800 of 98 081                at L=167      (21.2%)

and its share GROWS with length, being linear in L where the histogram form
is flat.

$ENIGMA_JORDER=ic ranks the scan by the index of coincidence instead,
computed from the co-occurrence table in O(26) per move: build T once (it
depends on rows and ciphertext, not the board), take n for the current board
in 26 columns, then apply each toggle's 2-4 column delta.


WHY THE SAVING IS 11% AND NOT 22%
---------------------------------

The 22% figure is for `-S f10`, a bare fused target.  The RECOMMENDED
schedule is `k4f10`, whose k4 pre-pass is a low-order stage and therefore
already on the histogram path -- probe_toggle is O(26) there and there is
nothing to replace.  Only the f10 target stage's scans are affected, and they
are about half the run.  On a single -S f10 fixture at L=167 the same switch
takes 7 535 643 plugboards scored to 5 546 450, i.e. -26%.

So the honest number depends on the schedule, and 11% is the one for the
recipe people are told to use.


THE FIRST SEED OVERSTATED IT, WHICH IS WHY THERE ARE TWO
--------------------------------------------------------

Seed 4242 at L=167 read +1.68pp with 305 exact against 294, McNemar z=1.68 --
suggestive, and the sort of result it is tempting to report.  Seed 99 reads
+0.68pp with 312 against 311.  Pooled: +1.18pp, z=1.29, p about 0.20.

Not established.  The lean is consistently positive across both seeds and
both lengths (-0.45, +0.39, +1.68, +0.68), so there may be a small real gain,
but 1600 trials cannot resolve it and the claim here is level recovery.


WHY THIS IS NOT OBVIOUSLY A GOOD TRADE, AND WHY IT STILL IS
-----------------------------------------------------------

Ordering by IC is not ordering by the target: the climb visits moves in a
different sequence and can converge elsewhere.  CLAUDE.md records that a
STATIC informed order was measured DOWN, because it destroyed the restart
diversity -J depends on.  This order is still rebuilt per restart from the
perturbed board, so it keeps that property -- which is the likely reason it
costs nothing.

That the order can be approximated at all is consistent with --biased-random's
finding that a single plug's IC carries real signal (12.1% of true plugs in
the top 10 of 325 against 3.1% by chance at L=100).  The order only has to be
approximately right: it is a visit sequence for first-improvement, not a
decision.


WHAT IS NOT MEASURED
--------------------

  * PROSE.  Telegraphic German only.  CLAUDE.md records that scoring results
    do not transfer between the two writing styles.
  * OTHER TARGETS.  -q and -a pay the same full-decode scan and should behave
    the same way, but were not run.
  * OTHER BUDGETS.  -R 8 only.  The pre-pass ordering question was
    restart-budget dependent (SS6.10), so this should not be assumed to hold at
    high -R.
  * WHETHER THE SAVING CONVERTS.  The obvious next question is whether 11%
    more restarts at matched wall time buys breaks.  On the documented restart
    curve 11% more -R is worth well under a point, so the honest expectation
    is "a little, but not much".
  * THE FULL SWEEP.  Rotor key given throughout, so this measures the
    plugboard tier only -- which is where the move order lives, but not what
    an operator runs.

DEFAULT REMAINS OFF.  The evidence supports "same recovery, 11% cheaper on
one recipe at one budget on one writing style".  That is a good reason to
keep the switch and a thin reason to change what everybody gets.
