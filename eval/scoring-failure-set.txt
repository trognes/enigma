WHAT THE SET SHOWS
------------------

1. Operational-length scoring failures EXIST but are RARE: 3 of 18 messages
   in the 107-167 band. At this length most misses are search failures, which
   -R fixes; these three are the residue that no search count reaches.

2. They are READABLE German at the true key -- the "wordless" label in
   results-word-segment.txt is a word-LIST gap (Russian place names absent
   from a German dictionary), not a garbled decrypt.

3. They score below the bar for two compounding reasons, both visible in the
   plaintext: TRANSMISSION GARBLES (KCKOREWO, BESPREQUNGNGQ) and
   OFF-DISTRIBUTION VOCABULARY (Tschedinowa, Kokorewo, Ludsen, Portschow) that
   the German quad tables score as near-random.

4. EVERY ONE CARRIES A DOUBLING, and this is structural, not coincidence: the
   rare place name that makes the message score badly is exactly what the
   operator doubled for error-correction. So at operational length,
   "scoring failure" and "has a doubling" coincide.

CONSEQUENCE FOR A NEURAL-NET / WORD-LIST RE-RANKER
--------------------------------------------------

BUT THE REPORT NEEDS THE CLIMB TO SUCCEED ON THE RIGHT ROTORS FIRST, and that
splits these three from EGERF -- see results-climb-at-truekey.txt. At their
own true rotor key, board hidden, FTNBK/HBNVE/ALWOK recover 100% (plaintext
appears, --doubling-report fires on LUDSEN X LUDSEN, KOKOREWO X KCKOREWO,
TSCHEDINOVA X TSCHEDINOVA), while EGERF recovers only 7% -- the most garbled
of the four, its board does not recover even given the right rotors, so no
plaintext ever surfaces and NO plaintext-side method (report or LM) can reach
it. So of these four, three are caught by the shipped report and one is below
the recovery line entirely. An LM re-ranker's only marginal population over
the report is the readable-German-WITHOUT-a-doubling scoring failure that ALSO
recovers at the true key -- empty in this band of the corpus.

The genuinely stuck messages are the OTHER kind: too short or too garbled to
recover readable plaintext at the true key at all. Those sit below the
information floor, where no plaintext-side method -- word list, doubling
report or LM -- has anything to read.
