The probe microkernel on the Mac mini M1 -- metal/DESIGN.md 17.6 (ii)
=====================================================================

metal/probe_host.cc via $ENIGMA_GPU_PROBE=1 on metal/enigma-metal.  One
toggle probe -- apply a toggle, score the board -- repeated 64 times per
unit under one deterministic toggle sequence, in five arms doing
identical work; probes per second, the min of three ~0.4 s runs, every
unit's checksums verified against the tool's own score_components.
L=105 (the earlier fixture, 107 letters less two), -f, 64 lanes per
threadgroup.


Run 1 -- ONE start board, which is a confound; kept for what it says
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  arm                      units     probes/s   vs lane   cap  check
  lane (today)            139456     70967882     1.00x   512  ok
  group K=32 shuffle       56044     32852392     0.46x  1024  ok
  group K=32 packed        58118     32686511     0.46x   896  ok
  group K=16 packed       102368     49890343     0.70x   896  ok
  group K=8 packed        149840     63523033     0.90x   896  ok

THE INSTRUMENT IS VALID.  All five arms compiled at the first attempt
and every unit of every arm reproduced score_components bit for bit --
the packed board, the nine-word histogram, the boundary quads by
shuffle-up and the xor butterfly all agree with the CPU across 64
toggles from a ten-pair kicked board.  The register caps are per
pipeline: the lean shuffle arm reads the full 1 024, the packed arms 896
(five board words and nine histogram words held by name), and the lane
arm 512 -- against the climb kernel's 384, which says the climb's scan
machinery around the scorer is what costs the rest.

EVERY GROUP ARM IS SLOWER THAN THE LANE, monotone in K.  Fit
K*T(K) = c*L + K*(R*log2 K + F) to the three packed rows (per-character
work plus per-probe reductions and fixed cost, both scaling with K in
lane-time): per-character 0.89, R = 0.006, F = 0.009 in units of the
lane arm's probe time.  So the per-character step in the group shape
costs 0.89 of the lane's -- shuffles and packed-ALU lookups replaced the
thread-memory reads and the step barely moved -- and the reductions are
what separate K=8 from K=32.  Even with the reductions free the group
shape would reach ~1.1x the lane.

THE CONFOUND.  Every unit ran the SAME board under the same toggles, so
in the lane arm all 32 lanes of a simdgroup read the same rows[] entry
and gathered the same table cell at every character -- a broadcast and
one cache line, where a climb's 32 lanes hold 32 boards and touch 32.
The group arms gain nothing from it, their lanes reading different
positions by design; so the bias is one-way, against them.  The tell is
the lane arm's absolute rate: 71M probes/s against 22M for the
fixed-pass climb doing nominally the same scoring (results-gpu-
ablation-m1.txt run 4: 44.8 ns per probe at L=107), a 3.2x that the cap
(512 against 384) does not cover.  Either the broadcast is worth ~2x to
the lane arm, or the climb's scan machinery costs twice the scoring
itself.  Both matter and this run cannot tell them apart.

Fixed by giving unit u start board u % 64, the 64 boards being the
kicks for (key 0, restart 0..63), so a simdgroup holds 32 boards as a
climb does, with 64 oracles in place of one.  Run 2 is that.
