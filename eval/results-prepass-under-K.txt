DOES -K's IC MOVE ORDER SUBSTITUTE FOR THE IC PRE-PASS?  HALF OF IT
====================================================================

  python3 eval/prepass_ab.py --length 100 --trials 10000 --restarts 8 \
          --arms m4f10 k4f10 --climb-rule {J,K} --threads 1 --jobs 3 \
          --seed {4242,99}

  -c --polish -l wehrmacht -R 8, L=100, 10-pair board hidden, rotor key given,
  authentic HG Nord decrypts.  40 000 paired trials in total: 10 000 per seed
  per climb rule, and the SAME trials in both rules (the trial set is drawn
  from the seed before anything runs), so the two gaps are directly
  comparable.  JUDGED ON BREAK50.

THE QUESTION.  -K ranks the climb's move-ordering scan by the index of
coincidence; the k4 pre-pass fuses mono with IC.  Both inject IC information,
so -K might make the cheaper mono pre-pass adequate -- in which case m4f10
with -K would be the best combination and the k4f10 recommendation would need
revising.

ANSWER: the mechanism is real and it is worth half the gap, which is not
enough.  k4f10 still wins under -K by 2.67pp (z = -5.6 per seed).


THE FOUR CELLS
--------------

  seed  rule    m4f10    k4f10     gap        z      mean %-correct diff
  4242   -J    34.13%   39.26%   +5.13pp   -10.34         -4.89
  99     -J    32.43%   38.30%   +5.87pp   -11.93         -5.53
  4242   -K    36.77%   39.41%   +2.64pp    -5.46         -2.72
  99     -K    35.74%   38.44%   +2.70pp    -5.71         -2.58

  mean gap under -J:  +5.50pp
  mean gap under -K:  +2.67pp
  -K NARROWS IT BY    2.83pp  (51%)

Both seeds agree within each rule (5.13/5.87 and 2.64/2.70), and the
difference of differences is 2.49pp and 3.17pp -- the same story twice.
Taking each gap's SE from its own z (0.50pp and 0.48pp), the DD is ~2.8pp
against ~0.69pp, i.e. z ~ 4.1; and that is conservative, since the two runs
share their trials and a properly paired SE would be smaller.


READ DOWN THE COLUMNS -- THAT IS WHERE THE MECHANISM IS
---------------------------------------------------------

               -J        -K       change
  m4f10      34.13%    36.77%     +2.64
             32.43%    35.74%     +3.31
  k4f10      39.26%    39.41%     +0.15
             38.30%    38.44%     +0.14

-K LIFTS m4f10 BY ~3pp AND k4f10 BY NOTHING.  That is exactly the
substitution hypothesis: an IC-ranked move order supplies something the k4
pre-pass already supplies, so it helps only the schedule that lacks it.  It is
also the same split the 24-cell -J/-K grid found (eval/results-jorder.txt: -K
gains on f10 and m4f10, nothing on k4f10 and i4f10) -- but that grid could not
say whether the lift was enough to REORDER the schedules, because it never
compared schedules to each other.  This does, and it is not.

So the two are PARTLY redundant, not additively independent and not
interchangeable.  Half the k4 pre-pass's advantage is information -K can also
supply; the other half is not.


A FREE CROSS-CHECK OF BOTH INSTRUMENTS
----------------------------------------

The -J arm's mean %-correct gap is -4.89pp [-5.73, -4.04] and -5.53pp
[-6.36, -4.69].  CLAUDE.md records +4.95pp [+3.06, +6.84] for these same two
schedules at this same length, measured by a DIFFERENT harness
(eval/results-monoic-endtoend.txt).  Agreement to 0.06pp on the first seed is
good evidence that neither measurement is broken.

That also settles a temptation recorded and resisted earlier in this work: the
-K gap (2.65pp mean) was first compared against that documented 4.95pp, which
would have been a cross-run comparison of the kind that has misled this
project repeatedly.  Running the -J arm in the same harness gives the same
conclusion for the right reason, and the cross-run agreement is now a check
rather than the evidence.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE
--------------------------

  * k4f10 REMAINS THE RECOMMENDED PRE-PASS for telegraphic traffic, now
    confirmed under the recommended climb rule rather than only under -J.
  * -K remains recommended: it costs nothing on k4f10 and it is what lifts
    m4f10, so a caller who has not tuned the schedule benefits most.

WHAT IS NOT MEASURED
---------------------

  * ONE LENGTH.  L=100 only.  The documented k4f10-over-m4f10 margin GROWS
    with length (+0.52pp at L=60, +4.95 at 100, +8.31 at 167), so at L=167
    -K's ~2.8pp of narrowing would close proportionally less of it -- the
    conclusion should get stronger, not weaker, but that is inference.
  * ONE RESTART BUDGET.  -R 8.  CLAUDE.md records pre-pass questions as
    restart-budget dependent.
  * PROSE.  Telegraphic German throughout, as the k4f10 recommendation itself
    is.
