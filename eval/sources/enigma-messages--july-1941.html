German Army Enigma Messages
from July 1941, Batch A, Incoming (Red) Messages.

The 48 cipher text messages below are Enigma messages from the German Army Collection for the month of July 1941. The messages, which we have labeled Batch A because they seem to be different from the other July messages, have never been broken. Most of the messages in Batch A are in hand cipher, probably a variation of Doppelkastenschlüssel, and most of the Enigma messages are very short. We therefore decided to attack the April 1945 cipher text, the Flossenbürg messages, instead of attempting to break the July messages from this batch. Red message numbers means incoming messages while blue message numbers are used for outgoing messages. All the messages in this batch are red, hence they are all incoming or received messages.

Some of the messages are probably in the Enigma key of the previous day. An example of this is Nr. 157 which was received at 01:00 on 10 July 1941, but where the header shows that the message was prepared at 23:13. Sometimes there also seems to have been a confusion between AM/PM time and 24 hours time. Nr. 158 shows the time in the header as 11:00 while it is more likely it should have been 23:00. A clear example of this confusion is Nr. 107 where the time in the header has been corrected from 08:10 to 20:10.

For the non German speakers, here is a short explanation of some of the German terms used in the message header:

  • Aufgenommen am: = Received on:
  • Befordert am: = Sent on:
  • Uhr: = Time:
  • Durch: = By (operator):
  • Funkspruch Nr.: = Radio Message No.:
  • Von: = From:
  • An: = To:
  • Vermerke: = Remarks:
  • Absendende Stelle: = Transmitting Station:

For further information about our codebreaking project see our Web Portal: Breaking German Wehrmacht Ciphers.
