THE LAGGED-KAPPA TERM, MEASURED END TO END -- SAME VERDICT AS X-STRUCTURE
=========================================================================

  python3 eval/kappa_ab.py --trials 300 --lengths 40 60 100 --mu 0.3 1.0

  300 paired trials per length, 10-pair board hidden, rotor key given,
  -c -K --polish -S k4f10 -f -l wehrmacht -R 8.  JUDGED ON BREAK50, McNemar
  exact on the paired discordants.  No fitted table and nothing held out: the
  per-lag null is the candidate's own expected match rate.

RESULT: neutral where the term is weak, catastrophic where it is strong, and
no window between.

  L    mu=0     mu=0.3          mu=1.0
  40    0/300    1/300 p=1.00    1/300 p=1.00
  60   23/300   21/300 p=0.824    6/300 p=0.000
 100   90/300   93/300 p=0.766   14/300 p=0.000

At L=100 mu=1 the discordants are 79 only-base against 3 only-mu.  At mu=0.3
there is real churn (21/24 discordant at L=100) and zero net -- the term
changes WHICH trials break, not how many.


THE EXIT-BOARD-INVARIANCE ARGUMENT DID NOT SAVE IT, WHICH IS THE FINDING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

X-structure was gameable through the exit plugboard (move a plug, relocate
every X), and one could hope kappa's equality-only construction -- invariant
to the exit application by algebra -- closed that channel.  It closed HALF of
it.  The entry application still shapes which pre-core letters collide, and
at mu=1 the climb exploits that channel alone hard enough to take L=100 from
90 breaks to 14.  A term the search can influence AT ALL is a term the search
will optimise INSTEAD of the language, whenever it is weighted strongly
enough to matter.

And the screen was again no protection: kappa passed it MORE cleanly than
X-structure did (every length improving under all three generator sets at
mu=0.3, the statistic alone out-ranking the full fused model 56-70% against
40-47%).  Offline, the term faces decoys never selected against it; end to
end, each arm climbs under its own mu.  Two terms have now passed that screen
and both inverted or nulled end to end.


WHERE THIS LEAVES THE SCORING QUESTION
---------------------------------------

Four levers measured down in one investigation: the mixture weights (~1pp,
below resolution), lambda (unjudgeable offline; documented as a climb effect,
not discrimination), X-structure (inverts), and lagged kappa (nulls or
inverts).  The two structural failures bracket the design space -- one term
gameable via the exit board, one invariant to it and still gamed via the
entry board.  The scoring-failure floor on short telegraphic messages is
behaving like a genuine information limit for single-message statistics
added to the target score, which is what CLAUDE.md's "-a is near-optimal on
both scoring axes" already implied for prose.

WHAT REMAINS UNTRIED is not another term but another ARCHITECTURE:
  * REPORTING signals (the --doubling-report shape), which enter no ranking
    and so cannot be gamed -- kappa's top-3 peaks would slot into that
    pattern as a "this converged decrypt self-repeats like language" flag;
  * JOINT scoring across messages sharing a day key, where the board must
    make ALL traffic language-like at once -- an information multiplier no
    single-message term can match.

The implementation is reverted; src/ is byte-identical to dev.  The screen,
the implementation discipline (KP=false proven 374/374 instruction-identical,
40/40 numerical cross-check) and both harnesses are retained.
