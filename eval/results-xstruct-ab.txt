THE X-STRUCTURE TERM, MEASURED END TO END -- IT DOES NOT SURVIVE THE SEARCH
===========================================================================

  python3 eval/xstruct_ab.py --trials 300 --lengths 40 60 100 \
          --mu 30 100 300 --restarts 8 --seed 21

  300 paired trials per length, authentic HG Nord decrypts, 10-pair board
  hidden, rotor key given, -c -K --polish -S k4f10 -f -l wehrmacht -R 8.
  Gap table fitted on a HELD-OUT half of the corpus (37 messages, 231 gaps);
  plaintexts drawn from the other 38.  JUDGED ON BREAK50.

RESULT: the term is neutral where it is weak and HARMFUL where it is strong.
There is no mu that helps.

  L    mu=0    mu=30         mu=100              mu=300
  40   6/300   5/300 p=1.00  3/300  p=0.453      0/300  p=0.031
  60  19/300  20/300 p=1.00  10/300 p=0.035      2/300  p=0.000
 100  91/300  94/300 p=0.75  60/300 p=0.000      9/300  p=0.000

(McNemar, exact, on the paired discordants.  At L=100 mu=300 the discordant
pairs are 85 only-base against 3 only-mu.)


THE OFFLINE PROBE HAD THE WRONG SIGN, NOT MERELY A LOOSE BOUND
---------------------------------------------------------------

eval/results-xstruct.txt reported, at mu=300, truth-wins rising from 3.8% to
27.5% at L=40 and from 40.0% to 47.5% pooled -- and it held under BOTH
generator sets, which is the check that killed the lambda result.  End to end
the same setting takes break50 at L=40 from 6 to ZERO.

The probe's stated caveat was that its decoys came from n-gram and IC models,
none of which optimises X structure, so the term faced a pool never selected
against it and the numbers were an UPPER BOUND.  That was right about the
direction of the bias and wrong about its size: it is not a bound that
shrinks, it is a bound that CHANGES SIGN once the search can optimise the
term.  A climb under the augmented score finds boards whose X land at
plausible word lengths, and at mu = 100-300 the term is strong enough that
optimising it beats optimising the language.

CLAUDE.md already records this failure mode for the kick-ranking probe --
"treat an offline seeding probe as justifying a recovery run, never as
evidence of its sign".  This is the same lesson in a second place, and this
time the offline evidence was much stronger: two generator sets, monotone in
length, and a mechanism.  NONE OF THAT SUBSTITUTES FOR LETTING THE SEARCH
ATTACK THE TERM.

WHY BOTH ENDS FAIL, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES THIS COMPLETE.  At mu = 30 the term
contributes ~0.3 to a per-symbol score of ~-8 and changes nothing (p >= 0.75
at every length; the probe agreed, reading 39.6% against 40.0%).  At mu = 300
it contributes ~3 and dominates.  There is no window in between: mu = 100 is
already significantly negative at two of three lengths.  The term is either
too weak to matter or strong enough to be gamed.

AND THE HARM GROWS WITH LENGTH, which is the opposite of what the idea
predicted.  The rationale was that short messages give n-grams too little to
work with, so structure should help most there.  At mu = 100 the loss is
3/300 at L=40 but 31/300 at L=100 -- because a longer message has more X, so
more gap evidence for the climb to exploit in the wrong direction.


WHAT IS AND IS NOT RULED OUT
-----------------------------

RULED OUT: adding X-gap likelihood to the target score as a weighted term.
Both ends of the mu range are covered and the middle is negative.

NOT RULED OUT, and untested: using the statistic as a REPORTING signal rather
than a score term -- the shape --doubling-report already has, where a
confirmation cannot promote a wrong key because it enters no ranking.  That is
also how the doubled-word evidence was measured DOWN as a score bonus and kept
as a report (ENHANCEMENTS.md item 5(e)), so the precedent for this exact
pattern is already in the tool.  Nothing here bears on it either way.

The implementation is reverted; src/ is byte-identical to dev.  What it cost
to learn is recorded above the revert in the commit, including the two bugs
the numerical cross-check caught before the run -- a triplicated gap loop
whose pre-roll copy dropped a gap, and a tail-bin null worth ~1.5 log units
per tail gap.  Both would have biased this measurement had they survived.
