AN X-DELIMITER STRUCTURE TERM -- PASSES THE SCREEN, NEEDS AN END-TO-END TEST
============================================================================

  python3 eval/xstruct_probe.py --trials 80 --lengths 40 60 100 \
          --restarts 64 --mu 0 1 3 10 30 100 300 [--generators <letters>]

  240 trials, authentic HG Nord decrypts, 10-pair board, rotor key given.
  Score = the shipped fused model + mu * (X-gap log-likelihood ratio).

THE IDEA.  Telegraphic German uses X as a word separator, so a correct decrypt
carries structure an n-gram model sees only through a 4-character window: the
GAPS between consecutive X's are word lengths.  Measured over the 75 authentic
messages, X is 6.2-6.7% of letters, gap median 7.5-9, and P(gap = 0) = 0.5%
against the 6.7% that random placement at the same rate would give.  It needs
no table beyond a gap histogram and is garble-robust -- one corrupted letter
moves one gap, where a high-order n-gram loses a whole window.

RESULT: the direction is stable, the short-message gain is large, and there is
a real cost at long lengths.  Truth-wins %, pooled generators i/t/q/a/f:

    mu     all    L=40    L=60   L=100
  0 (shipped)  40.0%    3.8%   33.8%   82.5%
    100        45.4%   15.0%   45.0%   76.2%
    300        47.5%   27.5%   46.2%   68.8%

AND IT HOLDS UNDER BOTH GENERATOR SETS, which is what the lambda result in
results-score-weights.txt failed:

  generators      mu=0            mu=100          mu=300
  q t (no IC)   47.1% / 18.8%   51.1% / 27.5%   49.8% / 36.2%
  i f (IC)      42.1% /  3.8%   50.0% / 16.2%   54.2% / 27.5%
                (all / L=40)

Both sets improve, at both mu.  The lambda comparison moved 24 points between
these same two sets; this one moves in the same direction in both.

THE STATISTIC ALONE IS COMPARABLE TO THE WHOLE N-GRAM MODEL.  Ranking purely
on X-gap structure puts the truth above every decoy in 43.8% / 49.8% / 50.4%
of trials (pooled / q t / i f), against the full fused model's 40.0% / 47.1% /
42.1%.  A table-free gap histogram discriminates about as well as four orders
of n-grams plus IC -- on this decoy pool, which is the whole caveat below.


THE BIAS THIS PROBE CANNOT REMOVE
----------------------------------

Every decoy is the argmax of some n-gram or IC model.  NONE of them optimises
X structure, so the new term faces a pool that was never selected against it.
That is the same mechanism that made lambda look catastrophic in the weight
probe, with the sign reversed: there it penalised an existing term, here it
flatters a new one.

It cannot be controlled away offline, because generating an X-aware decoy
requires a climb under the X-augmented score, i.e. the implementation.  So
these numbers are an UPPER BOUND, and the honest verdict is that the term
earns an implementation and a paired recovery A/B -- not that it works.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE VERDICT: if, once implemented, a climb under the
augmented score finds boards that game the gap structure (a wrong board whose
X's happen to land at plausible word lengths), the gain collapses.  Nothing
here rules that out.


TWO IMPLEMENTATION NOTES THE PROTOTYPE ALREADY PAID FOR
--------------------------------------------------------

A RAW LOG-PROBABILITY CANNOT BE THE STATISTIC.  A decrypt with fewer than two
X's has no gap to score, and whatever constant stands in for it is then
compared against real texts' genuine negative scores.  Measured: a neutral of
0.0 against a truth scoring -1.59 made the term PENALISE the truth in 100% of
trials, and the probe reported the term as catastrophic.  The fix is a
LIKELIHOOD RATIO against random X placement at the candidate's OWN X rate --
no X's then gives 0 because there is no evidence either way, and being X-rich
earns nothing (the monogram model already prices that).

THE CORPUS MUST BE HELD OUT AND MUST NOT BE CONCATENATED.  The plaintext is
drawn from the same 75 messages the gap distribution is fitted on, so the fit
excludes the source message (leave-one-message-out).  And excerpts come from
ONE message: concatenating the corpus fabricates a gap across every message
boundary, which is harmless for n-grams and fatal for a statistic that IS the
gap distribution.

ON THE SCALE OF mu.  The gap ratio is ~0.01 per symbol, so mu = 100
contributes ~1 to a per-symbol score of ~-8 -- the same order as the existing
IC term (lambda = 30 on an IC of ~0.05 contributes ~1.5).  mu = 100 is
therefore not an extreme setting in context, though it is far above the
weight a strict likelihood combination would give (mu = 1, which does
nothing).  The term carries ~0.6 log10 units of evidence per message against
the n-gram model's 6-30, so it is being deliberately over-weighted relative to
its likelihood content -- which is exactly what the non-adversarial pool
permits and an end-to-end test would challenge.
