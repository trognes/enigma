DOES -K's WIN TRANSFER TO PROSE?  YES, AND IT LOOKS THE SAME
=============================================================

  python3 eval/jorder_ab.py --tsv --trials 1500 --length L --restarts 32 \
          --schedule S --corpus C --language LANG --jobs 3

  12 cells: {english, german} x {f10, m4f10} x L = {40, 60, 100}, 1500 paired
  trials each -- 18 000 pairs.  10-pair board hidden, rotor key given, -f
  target, -R 32.  Arms differ only by -J against -K.  JUDGED ON BREAK50.

  english corpus: eval/corpus-tune-phase-ab.txt (136 KB of prose).
  german  corpus: eval/corpus-german-prose.txt (see the CAVEAT below).

VERDICT: prose reproduces the telegraphic finding rather than reversing it.
Pooled Stouffer Z = -6.08 over 12 cells, +314 breaks of 18 000.  NOT ONE CELL
IS SIGNIFICANTLY AGAINST -K.

THE SPLIT IS BY LENGTH, NOT BY LANGUAGE OR SCHEDULE
-----------------------------------------------------

  L=40   +0.46   nothing
  L=60   -4.55   -K
  L=100  -6.44   -K

Both languages favour -K, both schedules favour -K, and the gain rises
monotonically with message length in every arm.  That is the same shape the
telegraphic grid showed (f10 z = -0.37/-1.00 at L=60 rising to -4.70 at
L=120), so the effect is a property of the move order and not of the writing
style -- which is what one would expect of a change whose mechanism is that
the replaced scan is linear in L while its replacement is flat.

AT L=40 IT IS A WASH, NOT A LOSS.  The four L=40 cells read +0.93, +1.23,
-1.24, +0.00 -- scattering around zero.  Two English cells alone looked like a
consistent short-message penalty and were written up mid-run as a concern; the
two German cells killed it.  That is the third time in this line of work a
two-cell sign has failed to survive a third cell.


WHAT IS SATURATED AND SHOULD NOT BE READ AS A WEAK EFFECT
-----------------------------------------------------------

German prose at L=100 breaks 1470/1500 = 98.0% on the -J arm.  With 30
failures left there is almost nothing for either arm to win, so its z of -1.85
(f10) is a ceiling effect rather than a fading effect.  The useful German
range here is L = 40..60; the useful English range is L = 60..100, English
prose being markedly harder at the same length (f10 L=40 breaks 4% against
German's 23%).


THE PLUGBOARD SAVING SPLITS EXACTLY AS ON TELEGRAPHIC
--------------------------------------------------------

  f10    -25 to -27%      m4f10   -12 to -13%

Same mechanism, same numbers: a low-order pre-pass stage is already O(26) via
the histogram path, so only the fused TARGET stage's scans are replaced, and
with no pre-pass at all every scan in the run is.  As always this is
plugboards scored, not wall time -- see eval/results-jorder-speed.txt, where
the wall figures are roughly two thirds of these.


CAVEAT: THE GERMAN CORPUS IS 476 CHARACTERS
---------------------------------------------

There is no German prose corpus of usable size in this repository.  The German
arm draws from the passage tests/crack_quality.py uses for CLANG=german -- the
same fixture every other German-prose claim here was measured on, which is why
it was used, but it is short.  At L=100 that is ~376 distinct offsets against
1500 trials, so excerpts repeat about four times.

What this does and does not cost.  Each trial still draws a FRESH key and a
FRESH board, and both arms see the identical trial, so the pairing and the
McNemar test are sound and the z values are not inflated by the repetition in
any obvious way.  What is limited is generalisation: the German result speaks
to that passage rather than to German prose at large.  The English arm has no
such limitation (136 KB, no repetition), and it agrees, which is the main
reason to believe the German cells are not an artefact of their fixture.


WHAT IS STILL NOT MEASURED
---------------------------

  * -q AND -a TARGETS.  Both pay the same full-decode ordering scan and should
    behave like the -f cells here, but were not run.
  * THE FULL SWEEP.  Rotor key given throughout, so this is the plugboard tier
    only -- which is where the move order lives, but not what an operator runs.
  * MATCHED COMPUTE.  Both arms get the same -R, so -K's saving is banked
    rather than spent on restarts.  See eval/results-jorder.txt.
  * L > 100 ON PROSE, and L < 40 anywhere.
