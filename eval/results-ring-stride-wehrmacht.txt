=== SUPERSEDED, and why -- do not quote these numbers ===

Every earlier measurement of this flag was contaminated. The --polish plugboard finisher
shared its enclosing `if` with the --ring-stride refinement and was not guarded by
opt_polish, so every strided run silently got a full plugboard hill-climb plus an
unconditional gain cascade that the K=1 baseline never got -- with no -c requested at all.
On a board supplied via -s it added spurious plugs and corrupted the decrypt. That is
where most of the apparent "cost of the stride" came from:

                        contaminated   ->   actual
    L=40 K=2   exact       38.0%              48.0%
    L=40 K=3   exact       38.0%              49.0%
    L=60 K=2   exact       66.0%              74.0%
    L=60 K=3   exact       63.5%              74.0%
    L=40 K=5   exact       32.5%              42.0%
    L=60 K=5   exact       60.5%              72.5%

and earlier still, under the +/-K/2 refinement window (also contaminated):

    40    2      200      38.5%            14%
    40    3      200      33.5%            21%
    60    2      200      64.5%            11%
    60    3      200      56.5%            21%

The widening comparison drawn from those two contaminated rounds is not necessarily void
-- both arms carried the same bug -- but the absolute costs against K=1 were wrong by
roughly an order of magnitude, and the "K>=3 is not recommended" verdict rested on them.
