  recov50  = the reported board decrypts >= 50% of message 1
  exact    = it is the true board, plug for plug
  mean%    = mean %-correct over ALL trials, the graded signal
  scoring  = share of NON-RECOVERED trials whose winner outscores
             the true board (a scoring failure, not a search one)
  visited  = a board clearing 50%% was echoed on a progress line,
             i.e. a climb PASSED THROUGH one and kept going up.
             A LOWER BOUND -- progress lines fire only above the
             run's high-water mark
  conv     = the answer did not read, but a CONVERGED board would
  promoted = of those, how many joint re-ranking over the search's
             own top-8 boards puts one of them first
  n good   = converged boards clearing 50%%
  best rank= where the best-SCORING one of them sits in the
             search's own ranking (so top-8 is visibly enough,
             or visibly not)
  board    = which good board the rank is of: 'true' the true
             one, 'any50' the best-scoring one clearing 50%%
  present  = share of trials whose converged boards include such
             a board; the rank columns are over those trials only
  top 8    = share of them inside the re-ranking window above
  p90      = 90th percentile rank, nearest-rank; blank under 20
             observations, where it would just be the worst one
  of n     = mean distinct converged boards, the scale a rank is
             read against
  plugs/10 = mean plugs the winning board shares with the truth
  lead     = its score minus the truth's, in LOG UNITS over the
             whole message (per-symbol score x length)
  start2   = share deriving message 2's TRUE start from the
             indicator -- six plugboard lookups, all must be right
