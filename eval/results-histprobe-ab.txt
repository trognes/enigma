WHAT THIS REPLACES, AND WHY THE OLD NUMBER WAS WRONG

PR #258 reported -3%/-5%/-9% for k4f10/m4f10/i4f10. Wrong in BOTH
directions: k4f10 understated 2x, i4f10 overstated, and the ordering
reversed between the first two. The true spread is 6.5..8.6%, so the
apparent 3x difference between schedules was mostly noise.

THE CAUSE WAS ONE FIXTURE PER CELL, not the drift it was blamed on.
A single ciphertext and key is one draw from a distribution whose
per-fixture spread is several points. Repetitions do not help: they
sharpen that one fixture's estimate. Fixtures buy the interval.

AND THE ATTEMPTED CORRECTION MADE IT WORSE. The raw reading was -3.9%
with a -1.5% control; the control was read as warm-up drift shared
linearly across the three arms, so the estimate was adjusted DOWN to
~-3% -- away from the true -6.5%, and further from it than the
uncorrected number had been. Correcting a one-draw estimate against a
control of the same size is fitting noise.
