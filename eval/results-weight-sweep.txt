THE CAP OF 30 IS TOO LOW AT L=200, AT THE OPERATING BUDGET.  lambda 45 and 65

BUT -R 0 OVERSHOOTS THE OPTIMUM BY 1.5-2x, AND THAT IS THE METHODOLOGICAL

A PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION WAS RESOLVED AGAINST ITS OWN FIRST BRANCH.  Before
run B the prediction recorded was: if -R 0 keeps rising through lambda 150 and

smoothest surface available -- and says nothing about lambda's optimum.  It
does not keep rising.  L=200 peaks near 90 and is -79 by 300, so the proxy is
biased rather than meaningless, which is the weaker and more useful of the two
branches.

THE PEAK MOVES WITH LENGTH, WHICH IS WHAT THE THEORY WANTS.  At -R 0, L=200
peaks near 90 and collapses by 300; L=300 sits on a broad plateau from 90 to
300 and is still positive at 300.  lambda scaling with L is the premise of the
shipped rule, and this is the first direct evidence for it above the cap.

PURE IC IS NOT A DISASTER AT L=300, AND THAT IS THE ODD ONE.  lambda 10000 is
effectively IC alone; it costs -40.6pp at L=200 but only -10.9pp at L=300, and
lambda 1000 costs -24.4pp against -2.2pp.  At three hundred letters the n-gram
half of -f is contributing very little -- the climb very nearly succeeds on
coincidence structure alone.  Not acted on; recorded because it bears on where
scoring work is worth doing at all.

THE ERROR STAYS WILDLY ASYMMETRIC, SO ERR LOW.  At L=200, overshooting to
lambda 1000 costs -488 per 2000 while the whole win being chased is +37 per
6000.  That asymmetry is the same one recorded for the short band and it is
the reason the cap exists at all.

NOT SHIPPED, AND THE REASON IS THE ONE THIS FILE KEEPS RE-LEARNING.  There is
exactly ONE length measured at the operating budget above the cap.  Lining up
the plateau midpoints gives lambda ~10 at L~65, ~20 at L=100 and ~55 at L=200,
an implied exponent near 1.5 -- steeper than the shipped linear rule (0.17*L
predicts 34 at L=200) AND than the sd-ratio argument (1.1*sqrt(L) predicts
33).  Both candidate theories undershoot in the same direction, which is
suggestive; three plateau midpoints, two of them soft, are not a rule.
Reading 5 above records the last time a line was fitted to exactly this kind
of evidence here, and it had to be withdrawn.

SCOPE, AND IT IS NARROW.  Operational procedure split long messages, so
lengths much above 300 are off-distribution for real traffic and are in any
case too easy to discriminate scoring variants (at -R 8 the pilot reads 98.7%
break at L=250 and 100% at L=400).  The cap binds from L=177.  So the band
where this question both matters and can be measured at the operating budget
is roughly L = 177-250, and closing it needs two or three more lengths in that
band at -R 8 -- not more trials at L=200.
