TABU AT HIGH RESTART BUDGETS -- bounded without implementing it
================================================================

  eval/tabu_probe.py --trials 100 --length 100 --restarts 100 148 1000 2040

  L = 100, 100 trials, rotor key GIVEN, 10-pair board hidden, authentic
  HG Nord decrypts, -c -J --polish -f -l wehrmacht -S k4f10 -T 1 -e 7,
  ENIGMA_SEED=0.  Boards read from --dump-all and canonicalised, so two
  spellings of one board compare equal.

       R  distinct/R  seed-dup   break  scoring  search    tabu ceiling
     100       0.722     16.4%      47        1      52          -R 138
     148       0.676     20.3%      51        1      48          -R 219
    1000       0.433     43.5%      66        2      32         -R 2309
    2040       0.346     53.2%      70        2      28         -R 5888


THE QUESTION
------------

CLAUDE.md closes tabu on --restart-tt (PR #100), which found near-total
basin diversity at --random 10 -- "a tabu visited-set has nothing to
forbid".  That is an -R <~ 64 result and the file says so, flagging the
high--R case as open: distinct converged boards per restart fall to 0.79
at -R 100 and 0.49 at -R 1000 (eval/restart_ladder.py), so at a high
budget most restarts DO rediscover a basin already found.

The premise is therefore true.  Whether forbidding helps is a different
question, and this measures it.


WHY THIS IS CHEAP -- TABU'S CEILING IS A RESTART COUNT
------------------------------------------------------

If R restarts converge to D distinct basins, a PERFECT tabu set -- one
that never revisits, and whose forced basins are drawn from the same
distribution as natural ones -- turns every duplicate into a fresh draw,
giving R distinct basins where the plain run got D.  That is exactly the
search a plain run of R * (R/D) restarts already performs.  So

    tabu's best case at R  <=  the measured gain from R to R*(R/D)

and both sides come out of the shipped binary.  The rows at 148 and 2040
are those ceilings for 100 and 1000 (2309 was out of budget; 2040 bounds
it from below and the curve there is nearly flat).


THE ANSWER: THE PREMISE HOLDS AND THE PAYOFF DOES NOT
-----------------------------------------------------

Duplication more than doubles across the range -- 27.8% of restarts
rediscover a known basin at -R 100, 65.4% at -R 2040.  The ceiling does
not move with it:

    at -R 100    47 breaks, perfect tabu <= 51    (+4 of 100)
    at -R 1000   66 breaks, perfect tabu <= 70+   (+4 of 100)

Those two facts are ONE fact.  Restarts duplicate more precisely because
the basin set is being exhausted, so a forced fresh draw reaches deeper
into an already picked-over set -- the ceiling flattens at the same rate
the duplication grows.  A visited-set is not harvesting untouched
territory; it is paying to skip the parts of the map that are cheap to
reach.


AND THE CEILING IS NOT FREE, WHICH IS THE STRUCTURAL HALF
---------------------------------------------------------

A basin is known only AT CONVERGENCE.  A visited-set therefore cannot
skip a duplicate climb -- it can only reject the result and kick again,
which costs a full extra climb.  So tabu at this level does not
SUBSTITUTE for compute, it IS compute, plus the bookkeeping.  The table
above is generous to it twice over: it prices tabu's gain at zero cost,
and it assumes the forced basins are as good as the natural ones when
the ones being forbidden are exactly the easy-to-reach ones.

The version that does save work is the one already shipped.
--seed-dedup tests BEFORE the climb (the stage-0 seed, a deterministic
function of key and restart), and it catches

    16.4% of restarts at -R 100     = 59% of all duplication present
    43.5% at -R 1000                = 77%
    53.2% at -R 2040                = 81%

Its share of the duplication RISES with budget.  What it cannot see --
two different seeds converging on one basin -- is 11.4pp at -R 100 and
13.2pp at -R 1000: roughly constant, shrinking as a share, and
unreachable before a climb by construction.


TWO THINGS TO KEEP SEPARATELY FROM THE VERDICT
----------------------------------------------

The precondition holds.  At L=100 the failures are SEARCH failures --
the true board never converged in any restart -- 52 of 53 at -R 100 and
28 of 30 at -R 2040, against 1-2 scoring failures.  This is not the L=40
regime where 90% of failures are scoring failures under the recommended
recipe (ENHANCEMENTS.md item 2), so the headroom that exploration would
have to convert genuinely exists.  The ceiling result is about tabu, not
about a floor sitting in front of it.

Restarts are still paying at the top of the range: 47 -> 66 -> 70 breaks
at -R 100 / 1000 / 2040.  The 1000 -> 2040 step is worth +4pp, in line
with eval/results-restart-ladder.txt's +8.9pp for 1000 -> 5000 at this
length.  Nothing here argues for a lower budget.


VERDICT
-------

Tabu stays closed, and now for a measured reason rather than a false
premise.  At the budgets where its premise is true, a perfect
implementation is worth <= +4 breaks per 100 trials, costs a full extra
climb per duplicate to collect, and is dominated by the 1.4x (at -R 100)
or 2.3x (at -R 1000) plain restart increase that buys the same thing
with no new code.
